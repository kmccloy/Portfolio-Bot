---
title: "Why I built a chatbot instead of a PDF"
date: 2026-08-17
excerpt: "A CV tells you what someone did. It rarely tells you how they think. So I built something that could actually answer back."
---

Most portfolios are static. A PDF, a LinkedIn profile, a neatly formatted list of jobs and dates. They tell you what someone *did*, in the past tense, from a safe distance.

They rarely tell you how someone actually *thinks* — how they'd handle a tricky coaching conversation, what they believe about AI and human development, or what they're bad at and why they're fine admitting it.

So I built something that could answer back.

This site runs on Claude, trained on my actual words — mostly voice notes, organised rather than rewritten, because typing it all out would have taken a decade I don't have. Ask it something real. See what comes back. If it's not compelling, that's useful information too.
